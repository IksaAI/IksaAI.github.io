02

Made for Mac and Windows

  • Only what you care about

    You set the threshold: the estimated intensity where you are, or the magnitude, that sends a notification.

  • See the waves

    Pick any earthquake and replay its P and S waves traveling from the epicenter to you on the map.

  • Intensities at a glance

    Open an earthquake and the map shows the intensity measured in each area. Japanese intensities come straight from the Japan Meteorological Agency, with a link to the official report.

  • Taiwan · Japan

    Choose the regions you follow; the ones you don't aren't downloaded at all.

  • Menu bar & system tray

    Close the main window and it keeps watching from the Mac menu bar or the Windows system tray, and still notifies you of new earthquakes.

  • Five languages

    繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, and Bahasa Indonesia, across the whole interface.

Your location never leaves your computer. It's only used to work out your distance from the epicenter and the estimated intensity where you are. Prefer not to share your location? Pick a city instead.

03

Install

  1. Download and open the DMG

    Download EQalert {{version}}, then open EQalert-{{version}}.dmg.

  2. Drag EQalert to Applications

    In the window that opens, drag the EQalert icon onto the “Applications” folder on the right.

  3. First launch: allow it to run

    EQalert isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it. That's expected. Do this once and it opens normally from then on.

    macOS 15 or later (Sequoia, Tahoe)
    1. Open EQalert from Applications. When the “Apple could not verify…” dialog appears, click Done.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to “Security.”
    3. Next to “EQalert was blocked…,” click Open Anyway, enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.
    macOS 14 Sonoma
    1. In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) EQalert and choose Open.
    2. Click Open again in the dialog that appears.
    Comfortable with Terminal? One line does it
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/EQalert.app

  4. Allow notifications and location

    Notifications tell you about new earthquakes; location is used to work out your distance from the epicenter and the estimated intensity where you are. You can adjust alert thresholds, sounds, and language later in Settings (⌘,).

  1. Download and run the installer

    Download EQalert {{exeVersion}}, then run {{exeName}}.

  2. “Windows protected your PC”: run anyway

    EQalert isn't code-signed yet, so Windows blocks it the first time. That's expected: click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Finish the setup wizard

    By default it installs for your account only, with no administrator rights needed. Once installed, EQalert stays in the system tray and starts when you sign in to Windows (you can turn this off in Settings).

  4. Allow notifications and location

    Notifications tell you about new earthquakes. Location is used to work out your distance from the epicenter and the estimated intensity where you are: turn on “Let desktop apps access your location” under Settings → Privacy & security → Location in Windows. Prefer not to share your location? Pick a city instead. You can adjust alert thresholds, sounds, and language later in Settings (Ctrl+,).
